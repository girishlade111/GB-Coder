Q&A - Ask Me Anything

Here are some questions I frequently get asked.

What are you most passionate about in technology?

I'm most passionate about the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and user experience. Building tools that are not just powerful but also intuitive and genuinely helpful to people is what drives me. I love the challenge of making complex systems feel simple and accessible.

How do you stay updated with the fast-paced changes in AI?

It's a constant effort! I dedicate time each day to reading research papers from sources like arXiv, following key figures and labs on social media, listening to tech podcasts, and, most importantly, hands-on experimentation. I believe you can't truly understand a new model or technique until you've tried to build something with it.

What's a project you're dreaming of building in the future?

My dream project is to create a fully personalized AI learning companion. Imagine an AI tutor that understands your learning style, adapts to your pace, and can generate custom lessons, examples, and projects on any topic, from coding to history. It would be the ultimate tool for lifelong learners.
